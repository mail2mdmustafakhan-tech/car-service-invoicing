The four older folders beside it -- carservice-source, Updated, Updated2
and Update3 -- are previous versions. Nothing has been taken from them.
Leave them alone until you have seen this folder run, then delete them
yourself whenever you like. From now on every update goes here, and here
only.


WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
--------------------

1.  Double-click  setup-git-once.bat
    Takes a few seconds. You only ever do this once.

2.  In that same window, type:   git push -u origin main
    This sends everything up to GitHub, which has been three updates behind.

3.  Double-click  run.bat
    The first run takes a minute or two while it installs what it needs.
    After that it is quick. The browser opens by itself.

STILL FOR YOU TO FILL IN
------------------------

Open Settings once and add the things only you know:

  * VAT registration number and CR number -- these are printed on every
    tax invoice and it is worth getting them right before you issue one.
  * Workshop phone number, email and address.
  * The Arabic in the repair contract: six numbered conditions and the
    authorisation paragraph were typed up from the photograph of your
    paper form. Read them through and correct anything that came out
    wrong. Settings -> Repair contract.


ONE THING WORTH A CONVERSATION
------------------------------

The software can now issue two kinds of bill: a tax invoice with VAT, and
a plain invoice without. Both are recorded and both show in your figures,
kept apart so your VAT return stays correct.

Which of your sales are *required* to carry a tax invoice under ZATCA is
a question for your accountant, not for software. Worth five minutes with
them before you start using the plain type as a matter of routine.
